Lab 3 – Task 3: What the Numbers Do Not Show

Objective

To identify three important non-financial factors that KhataPe should consider before making its lending decision and distinguish facts requiring verification from hypotheses.

Case Background

KhataPe is comparing two lending options:

- Option A: Personal loans
- Option B: Small-business loans

Financial calculations alone may not capture all the risks involved in choosing a loan portfolio.

Prompt Used

Identify three important non-financial factors KhataPe should consider before choosing between personal loans and small-business loans.

Consider:

1. RBI digital lending guidelines
2. Customer risk
3. Collection costs

Explain briefly why each factor matters.

Label each item as either:

- Fact to verify from an official source
- Hypothesis / consideration

Do not invent regulations or present unverified information as confirmed facts.

Actual AI Response – Recorded Findings

1. RBI Digital Lending Guidelines

AI Label: Fact to verify from an official source

KhataPe should check the applicable RBI digital lending directions to understand requirements relating to digital loan disbursement, customer disclosures, data privacy, borrower creditworthiness and grievance redressal.

Compliance matters because regulatory issues can affect the lender and its customers.

My Verification Note:

The Reserve Bank of India issued the Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 on 8 May 2025.

These directions cover borrower creditworthiness, disclosures, loan disbursal, grievance redressal and data requirements.

Official Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848

The existence and issue date of these directions are verified from the official RBI notification. However, this does not establish whether KhataPe complies with the applicable requirements. Their applicability must be checked according to the type of regulated lender and its lending arrangements.

Classification: Fact verified: RBI issued the Digital Lending Directions, 2025. Applicability to KhataPe requires further verification.

2. Customer Risk

AI Label: Hypothesis / Consideration

KhataPe should evaluate the repayment capacity and financial stability of individual borrowers and small-business borrowers.

Personal borrowers may experience income-related difficulties, while small businesses may experience changes in cash flow.

My Verification Note:

These are possible risks, not confirmed findings about KhataPe's customers. Actual borrower data and repayment records would be needed to evaluate them.

Classification: Hypothesis / Consideration.

3. Collection Costs

AI Label: Hypothesis / Consideration

KhataPe should compare the cost and difficulty of collecting repayments from personal borrowers and small businesses.

Different borrower groups may require different follow-up processes, staffing and collection efforts. Higher collection costs could reduce profitability.

My Verification Note:

The case does not provide collection-cost data. Therefore, differences in collection costs remain a hypothesis that KhataPe should investigate.

Classification: Hypothesis / Consideration.

My Observation

The AI identified three relevant non-financial factors. The RBI directions could be checked against an official source, while customer risk and collection costs require supporting business data.

Verifying that a regulation exists is different from confirming that a particular lender complies with it. The AI did not provide evidence proving that either loan option is better on these non-financial factors.

Conclusion

KhataPe should not choose a lending option based only on the simplified expected net result.

Before making a final decision, it should review applicable RBI requirements, assess borrower repayment risk and estimate collection costs.

These checks can support a more responsible and informed lending decision.

Note: No claim of verified RBI compliance is made in this task. The applicability of regulatory requirements to KhataPe must be checked separately.
