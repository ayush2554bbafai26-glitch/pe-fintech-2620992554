Lab 2 – Task 3: Polite Customer Support Replies

Objective

To use few-shot prompting to generate polite and non-committal customer support replies.

Tool Used

ChatGPT

Few-Shot Prompt

You are a customer support assistant for a bank. Write polite replies to customer complaints.

Examples:

Complaint: My payment failed. Please help.

Reply: We are sorry for the inconvenience. We are checking the issue and will share an update after verification.

Complaint: My money has not been credited.

Reply: We understand your concern. Our team will review the transaction details and assist you.

Now reply to three new complaints. Do not promise any refund date, amount, or guaranteed resolution.

New Complaints and AI Responses

1. Complaint: My refund has not arrived. When will I get it?

Reply: We understand your concern. Our team will check the refund status and share an update after verification.

2. Complaint: Money was deducted but the transaction failed.

Reply: We are sorry for the inconvenience. Our team will review the transaction details and assist you after verification.

3. Complaint: I cannot access my bank account.

Reply: We apologize for the inconvenience. Please share the issue you are facing so our team can verify your details and help you regain access.

Verification

## AI Output for 3 New Complaints

**Complaint 1:** My UPI payment failed but the amount was deducted.

**Reply:** We are sorry for the inconvenience. Please share the UPI transaction details through the bank's official support channel so our team can verify the transaction and assist you.

**Complaint 2:** I was charged twice for one purchase.

**Reply:** We understand your concern. Please share both transaction details through the bank's official support channel so our team can investigate the duplicate charge.

**Complaint 3:** My loan application status has not been updated.

**Reply:** We understand your concern. Please share your loan application reference number through the bank's official support channel so our team can check the status and provide an update.

## Verification

All three replies are polite and professional. None promises a refund, refund date, or specific amount.

All three replies are polite and professional. No specific refund date or amount is promised. The replies avoid guaranteeing a resolution before verification.
