# Lab 1 – Task 4: Translation and Email Writing

## Objective
To translate a failed-payment notification into Hindi without adding promises or dates, check it through back-translation, and draft a professional email to a partner bank.

## Tool Used
ChatGPT

## Date
18 November 2026

## Part 1: Translation

### Original English Notification

"Your payment could not be completed. If your account was debited, the amount will be processed for reversal according to the bank's applicable process."

### Prompt V1

Translate the following failed-payment notification into simple Hindi. Do not add any promise, refund date, guarantee, or information that is not present in the original message.

### Hindi Translation – Output V1

"आपका भुगतान पूरा नहीं हो सका। यदि आपके खाते से राशि कट गई है, तो बैंक की लागू प्रक्रिया के अनुसार राशि वापस करने की प्रक्रिया की जाएगी।"

### Revised Prompt V2

Review the Hindi translation against the original English notification. Make sure the meaning is unchanged. Do not add a refund deadline, guarantee, or any extra promise. Use simple and clear Hindi.

### Back-Translation

"Your payment could not be completed. If the amount was deducted from your account, the process of reversing the amount will be carried out according to the bank's applicable procedure."

### Manual Verification

The Hindi translation preserves the main meaning of the original notification. It does not promise an exact refund date or guarantee when the amount will be returned.

## Part 2: Email Writing

### Prompt V1

Draft a professional email of approximately 100 words from a fintech company's operations executive to a partner bank regarding a delayed settlement. Use placeholders for all IDs, amounts, and dates. Do not invent any real details or make unsupported promises.

### Email Output

**Subject: Request for Update on Delayed Settlement – [Settlement ID]**

Dear [Bank Partner Name],

We are writing to bring to your attention a delay in the settlement associated with [Settlement ID] for the amount of [Amount] scheduled on [Date].

We request you to kindly review the transaction status and share an update on the reason for the delay. Please also let us know if any additional information or documentation is required from our operations team.

We would appreciate your assistance in reviewing this matter and providing the current settlement status.

Regards,  
[Operations Executive Name]  
Operations Team  
[Fintech Company Name]

### Revised Prompt V2

Review the email to ensure it is professional, polite, and approximately 100 words. Keep placeholders for every ID, amount, date, and name. Do not invent details, promise a settlement date, or claim that the bank has confirmed anything.

## Verification Note

The Hindi translation was checked through back-translation to ensure that no extra promise or date was added. The email uses placeholders instead of invented settlement details and does not promise a settlement date.
