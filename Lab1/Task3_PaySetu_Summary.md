# Lab 1 – Task 3: PaySetu Summary Without Inventing

## Objective
To summarise fictional business information accurately without adding unsupported facts or conclusions.

## Tool Used
ChatGPT

## Date
18 November 2026

## Original Passage

"PaySetu, a fictional payments start-up, processed 4.2 lakh transactions in September and 5.1 lakh in October. Average transaction value fell from ₹640 to ₹590. Server downtime increased in October. No data is available on revenue or costs."

## Prompt V1

You are a careful financial analyst. Summarise the following fictional business information in exactly 3 factual bullet points. Then list what cannot be concluded from the given information. Do not invent facts, causes, revenue, profit, or costs.

## Output V1

**Summary:**
- PaySetu processed 4.2 lakh transactions in September and 5.1 lakh transactions in October.
- The average transaction value decreased from ₹640 in September to ₹590 in October.
- Server downtime increased in October compared to September.

**What Cannot Be Concluded:**
- The company's total revenue cannot be determined because revenue data is not available.
- The company's profit or loss cannot be determined because revenue and cost data are unavailable.
- The reasons for the increase in server downtime cannot be determined.
- The reasons for the decrease in average transaction value cannot be determined.
- The company's overall financial performance cannot be confirmed from the given information.

## Revised Prompt V2

Review your previous summary of PaySetu. Check that every statement is directly supported by the passage. Keep exactly 3 factual bullet points and a separate list of what cannot be concluded. Do not assume that higher transaction volume means higher revenue or profit. Do not invent reasons for changes in transaction value or server downtime.

## Output V2

**Summary:**
- PaySetu processed 4.2 lakh transactions in September.
- PaySetu processed 5.1 lakh transactions in October.
- The average transaction value decreased from ₹640 in September to ₹590 in October, and server downtime increased in October.

**What Cannot Be Concluded:**
- Total revenue cannot be determined because revenue data is unavailable.
- Profit or loss cannot be determined because revenue and cost data are unavailable.
- Higher transaction volume does not necessarily mean higher revenue or profit.
- The reasons for the decrease in average transaction value cannot be determined.
- The reasons for the increase in server downtime cannot be determined.
- The overall financial performance of PaySetu cannot be determined from the given information.

## Independent Calculation

**September:**

Transactions = 4.2 lakh = 4,20,000

Average transaction value = ₹640

Total transaction value = 4,20,000 × ₹640 = ₹26,88,00,000 = **₹26.88 crore**

**October:**

Transactions = 5.1 lakh = 5,10,000

Average transaction value = ₹590

Total transaction value = 5,10,000 × ₹590 = ₹30,09,00,000 = **₹30.09 crore**

**Comparison:**

Difference = ₹30.09 crore − ₹26.88 crore = **₹3.21 crore**

October's total transaction value was ₹3.21 crore higher than September's.

## Manual Verification

I independently calculated the total transaction value for both months. The calculations show an increase from ₹26.88 crore in September to ₹30.09 crore in October.

The AI correctly stated that revenue, profit, costs and the reasons for the changes cannot be determined from the passage. Total transaction value is not the same as company revenue or profit.

## Verification Note

The AI summary stayed within the information provided. The transaction value calculations were checked independently using the number of transactions multiplied by the average transaction value.
